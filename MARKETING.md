# CampusLoop: plan to get the first 1,000 students

Goal: 1,000 students who open the app at least twice in a week, in 8 weeks, with almost no money. The trick is **depth before breadth**: win one college completely (about 300 students) and use it as proof for the next ones. A busy board of 300 beats an empty board of 3,000.

## Principles
1. **One college at a time.** Pick the college where you already know people (RGUKT). Everything below is first run there.
2. **Give a reason to come back daily:** Daily quiz, streaks, Today card, weekly leaderboard. Tell people about these, not about "features".
3. **Make students look good, not you:** the share cards (quiz result, "my week") carry the brand into their stories for free.
4. **Trust first:** anonymous, moderated, no ads, free. Say it in every post.
5. **Ask for 10, not 1000:** 10 real students giving feedback weekly beats 1,000 downloads.

## Week by week
| Week | Goal | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Ready | Fill the legal placeholders, test sign-in on 3 phones, seed the board: post 20 real doubts/answers/notes yourself, add 10 papers (with permission) and a pinned welcome notice. |
| 1 | 30 students | Personal invites to your own batch, close friends and 5 class groups. Ask each person to invite 2 (the invite link gives them +3 days of Plus once payments are on). |
| 2 | 100 | Pitch 3 **class representatives** and 3 **club leads**: give each a staff account (events/notices) so they bring their clubs. Run a **7-day challenge**: "Top 10 on the weekly leaderboard get a certificate / a shout-out." |
| 3-4 | 300 | **Exam-season hook:** post "free paper vault + mock tests this week" (pin as a notice). Daily quiz in class groups at 8 pm. Print the invite link on one A4 poster per hostel/department (use the Copy link button). |
| 5-6 | 600 | Second college: use the 300 as proof. Visit one **student affairs / placement officer**: show the Weekly report and Campus Drives. Offer a free semester pilot. |
| 7-8 | 1,000 | Run a **college vs college battle** week between your two colleges (built in): share cards on Instagram stories. |

## Channels that work for Indian college students (free)
- **WhatsApp class and hostel groups** (best): one short message + link. Never spam; ask the admin first.
- **Instagram stories/reels:** 15-second screen recordings: "Ask a doubt anonymously", "I got 12/15 in the mock test", "Top of the weekly leaderboard". Use the **Share my week** and quiz share cards.
- **Telegram/WhatsApp channels for GATE/placement prep.**
- **Seniors and alumni:** they answer doubts and recruit juniors. Ask 10 seniors to answer 3 doubts each in week 1.
- **Clubs and fests:** use Events + RSVP for a real event (a coding contest). Events pull in non-users.
- **Poster + QR:** one clean poster at the canteen and library (add a QR made with any free QR site pointing at your invite link).

## Messages you can copy
**WhatsApp (class group):** "Hi all 🙏 We built CampusLoop for our college: ask doubts anonymously, daily quiz, previous-year papers, placement drives. Free, no ads, moderated. Join: <your link>. Tell me what to improve."
**Instagram caption:** "Ask doubts without fear. Daily quiz. Papers. Placement drives. One free app for our campus 💙 #CampusLoop"
**To a principal / student affairs:** "A free, moderated study community for your students that gives the exam cell and placement cell an official notice board, drives with eligibility lists, and a weekly engagement report. We would like to run a one-semester pilot at no cost."

## Numbers to track every Sunday
New students, students active in the last 7 days (the **Weekly report** tab), doubts asked and answered %, daily quiz participants, invite-link signups, top 3 things students asked for. Target for the first 1,000: **40% active weekly**, **60% of doubts answered within a day**.

## Money
- Keep everything students use today **free**. Plus is optional; never gate the board.
- Free marketing budget: ₹0. Optional ₹2,000-5,000: printing 50 posters, small prizes for the weekly challenge, a 1-month promo-code campaign (EXAM30).
- First revenue is more likely from **one college licence** than from student subscriptions. Use the pilots to get it.

## Risks and how to avoid them
- **Empty board:** seed it, and never launch a college with fewer than 20 real posts.
- **Abuse:** keep moderation active the first month; make 2 trusted students staff for each college.
- **Over-promising:** only advertise what works today. Test every feature on a real phone first.
- **Privacy:** never share student data with colleges beyond the Weekly report (counts only) and the placement list students opted into.
