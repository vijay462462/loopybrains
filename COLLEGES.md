# CampusLoop for colleges: the plan

What colleges pay for (in the order I would build and sell it). Prices are guesses to test.

| # | Feature | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Official notices**: the college posts timetables, results and circulars; students see a gold banner on the board | **Built** (admin dashboard › Notices) |
| 2 | **Verified-student-only board** (`requireVerified`, college email domains) so only real students post | **Built** (admin dashboard › Colleges) |
| 3 | **Moderation tools**: reports, hide/restore, device blocking, audit log | **Built** |
| 4 | **Paper vault** curated by the college | **Built** (admin dashboard › Papers) |
| 5 | **Own colours, subjects, clubs, branding** per college | **Built** |
| 6 | **Engagement report** (weekly active students, posts, doubts answered) emailed to the principal | Next |
| 7 | **College staff accounts** (a staff member who manages only their college) | **Built** (admin dashboard › College staff) |
| 8 | **Placement cell tools**: drives, eligibility, applicant list | Next |
| 9 | **Club and event management** with RSVP | Later |
| 10 | **Bulk email-domain verification** and a student count against the licence | Later |

## How to sell it
1. Pilot with 1 to 3 colleges free for a semester. Use the weekly numbers to show value.
2. Then ₹30 to ₹60 per student per year with a minimum (guess). Sell to the principal, the placement officer or the student affairs dean.
3. Show three things: fewer WhatsApp-group notices lost, verified students only, and a safe moderated space.

## Before you onboard a real college
- Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and a grievance contact (a lawyer should review them).
- Tell the college what data is stored (names chosen by students, posts, optional email) and that you do not sell data.
- Agree who answers reports (you, or a staff member once college admins exist).
