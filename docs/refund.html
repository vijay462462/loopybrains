Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. What you are buying

Loopy Brain Plus is a digital study plan for a fixed period (1 week, 1 month, about 4 months or 1 year). It does not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel; it simply ends when the period ends.

2. When we refund

  • Charged twice or wrong amount: full refund of the extra amount.
  • Paid but Plus did not switch on: we will switch it on, or refund in full if we cannot fix it within 7 days.
  • Yearly plan: full refund if you ask within 7 days of paying and have not used more than a few features. After that, no refund for the part already used.
  • Weekly, monthly and semester plans: no refund after the plan has started, except the cases above.

3. When we do not refund

If your account was blocked for breaking the Terms of Use, if you changed your mind after using the features, or if a free feature was always free.

4. How to ask

Write to v.bhaskar462@gmail.com from the email you paid with, with the payment id (you receive it from Razorpay) and a short reason. We reply within 3 working days.

5. How you get the money back

Approved refunds go to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually reach you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

6. If we stop the service

If we shut Plus down before your paid period ends, we will refund the unused part.

7. Contact
