Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Who we are

Loopy Brain is run by Loopy Brain (founder: Vijay M), Visakhapatnam, India ("we"). Contact: v.bhaskar462@gmail.com. This policy explains what we collect and why, in line with Indian law including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. What we collect

  • Anonymous sign-in id and device id, created automatically so the app works without a password.
  • Name you choose (it does not need to be your real name), optional profile photo, status and streak.
  • Your posts, replies, likes, stories, quiz and battle answers on your college board.
  • Email address, only if you choose to verify your email (any email works) or save a backup.
  • Study Lab data (flashcards, notes, tasks, planner), kept on your phone, and in our cloud only if you use backup.
  • Payment details: Razorpay handles payments. We receive only the payment id, plan and amount, and the email you used.
  • AI helper messages, sent through our server to the AI provider to produce an answer. We count how many questions you ask per day; we do not keep the text of your chats on our servers.
  • Survey answers and college requests that you choose to send, including an optional email or contact.
  • Reports and moderation records, including which device was blocked.

3. Why we use it

To run your college board, show your name and posts to students of your college, keep the app safe (reports, blocking, spam checks), give you Plus features, process payments, send the sign-in email link you ask for, and understand what students want. We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.

4. Who can see what

Your posts, chosen name, photo and status are visible to students signed in to the same college board. Your email is not shown to other students; only a verified tick is. Admins can see reports and the device id attached to a post, so they can block abusive devices.

5. Who we share with (service providers)

Google Firebase (database, sign-in, hosting of the service), Razorpay (payments), the AI provider (for the AI helper), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside India. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Posts stay until they are removed. Stories disappear from view after 24 hours. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires for accounts and tax. You can ask us to delete your data any time (see below).

7. Your rights

You may ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you, to withdraw your consent, and to complain. Write to v.bhaskar462@gmail.com with your name, college and the device or email you used. We will reply within 7 days and act within 30 days.

8. Children and teens

You must be at least 13. At RGUKT (Andhra Pradesh) the app is open only to B.Tech 2nd year students and above. At other colleges, if you are under 18 you need your parent or guardian's consent to use Loopy Brain. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, and we do not show targeted advertising to anyone. If you think a younger student is using it, write to us and we will remove the account.

9. Cookies and storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your college, name, theme and study data on your phone. We do not use advertising cookies.

10. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and protected by database security rules. No system is perfectly safe; please tell us at once if you notice a problem.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way we will tell you in the app.

12. Contact and Grievance Officer

Vijay M, v.bhaskar462@gmail.com, Visakhapatnam, India.

Not affiliated with any college

Loopy Brain is an independent student community. It is not run, owned or endorsed by any college or university, and names, initials and logos of colleges are used only to show which college a board is for, unless a college states otherwise.
